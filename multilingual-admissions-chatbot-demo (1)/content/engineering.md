## B.Tech courses and intake at Yashoda Faculty of Engineering

The Faculty of Engineering at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara offers Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programmes affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere. Current programmes, with their sanctioned intake and start year, are:

- Computer Science and Engineering — intake 180, started 2011, NAAC and NBA accredited, choice code 675724210
- Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering — intake 120, started 2011, choice code 675737210
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Science — intake 120, started 2022, choice code 675799510
- Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security) — intake 120, started 2024, choice code 675726010
- Robotics and Artificial Intelligence — intake 60, started 2024, choice code 675726610
- Cloud Computing — intake 60, started 2026, choice code 675762410
- Civil and Environmental Engineering (institute-level) — intake 60, started 2026, choice code 675762410
- Electrical Engineering — intake 60, started 2011, NAAC and NBA accredited, choice code 675729310
- Civil Engineering — intake 30, started 2011, choice code 675719110
- Mechanical Engineering — intake 30, started 2011, choice code 675761210
- Mechatronics Engineering — intake 30, started 2024, choice code 675762410

Computer Science and Engineering at Yashoda has the largest intake, while the newer AI, data science, cyber security, robotics and cloud programmes reflect the campus focus on emerging technology. The institute's autonomous curriculum is designed with industry input, and initiatives such as the Microchip Centre of Excellence, E-Plan Centre of Excellence and the Electric Vehicle laboratory give students hands-on exposure.

## M.Tech courses and intake

The Faculty of Engineering also offers Master of Technology (M.Tech) programmes affiliated to DBATU, Lonere:

- Mechanical Engineering — intake 12, started 2017
- Computer Science and Engineering — intake 18, started 2024
- Electric Vehicle Technology — intake 18, started 2026

## Departments and learning environment

Engineering students learn in well-equipped laboratories and smart classrooms, supported by the central library, central computer centre and high-speed internet on campus. The engineering departments run industrial tours, expert guest lectures and project competitions — students have participated in district-level events such as the Inspire Award project exhibition in Satara. Eligible diploma holders can seek direct second-year admission to B.Tech through the same centralised process.
