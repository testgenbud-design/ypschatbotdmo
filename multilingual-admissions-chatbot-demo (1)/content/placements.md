## How placements work at Yashoda

Placement support at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara runs through a dedicated Training and Placement Cell. The cell arranges campus interviews, training sessions, industrial tours and visits, and guest lectures by eminent personalities, so students meet industry well before graduation. The campus reports 300+ industry connects.

Figures published on the campus website include 105 companies visiting the campus and 2,033 total students placed, with a highest package of 22 LPA and an average package of 4 LPA. These are the campus's published numbers for the years it reports; placement outcomes vary by programme, year and the student's own performance.

## Recruiters and recent outcomes

Companies named in the campus's placement records include Capgemini, Atos, Byju's, Thermax, BASIC FIRST, Burns McDonnell, GaragePlug and Sandesh Solar & Electricals (Umbraj), among others visiting for campus drives. Pharmacy graduates have also moved into academia as assistant professors at pharmacy colleges, including the campus's own Faculty of Pharmacy. The campus has signed an MoA with the University of Jember, Indonesia for international academic cooperation, and has inaugurated industry-facing facilities such as the Microchip Centre of Excellence, E-Plan Centre of Excellence and the Electric Vehicle laboratory, which strengthen practical, placement-oriented learning.

## What students should know

The placement cell supports students with campus interviews, aptitude and soft-skill preparation and industry exposure — but no honest institution can promise a job to every student. Families are welcome to ask the placement cell for the latest year's placement summary when they visit the campus or speak to the admission office.
