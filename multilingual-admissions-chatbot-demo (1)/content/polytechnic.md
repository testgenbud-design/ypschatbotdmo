## Diploma (Polytechnic) courses and intake at Yashoda

The Faculty of Polytechnic at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara offers Diploma in Engineering programmes affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai. These three-year diploma programmes are open to students after SSC (10th standard) and give a direct route into technical careers or direct second-year B.Tech admission later. The polytechnic programmes, with intake and start year, are:

- Computer Engineering — intake 90, started 2022, choice code 675724510
- Civil Engineering — intake 60, started 2013, choice code 675719110
- Mechanical Engineering — intake 60, started 2013, choice code 675761210
- Electrical Engineering — intake 60, started 2014, choice code 675729310
- Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering — intake 60, started 2025, choice code 675737210
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — intake 60, started 2023, choice code 675799810
- Automation and Robotics — intake 60, started 2026
- Instrumentation and Control Engineering — intake 60, started 2026

## Why students from Satara district choose Yashoda polytechnic

For families across Satara district, the polytechnic at Yashoda offers a practical path: admission after 10th, hands-on laboratory work from the first year, MSBTE board examinations, and the same green campus facilities — library, computer centre, hostels, bus facility and canteen — as the degree students. The Faculty of Polytechnic runs its own induction programme (Arambh) for new diploma students, and diploma holders can progress to direct second-year B.Tech admission at the campus itself.
