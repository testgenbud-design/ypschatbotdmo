## Fees at Yashoda — always check the official fee page

Fees at Yashoda Technical Campus are approved by the Fees Regulating Authority (FRA), Government of Maharashtra, and differ by programme and admission year. The campus publishes its FRA-approved fee structures on its website — the FRA fee pages at yes.edu.in (FRA Fee Approved and Fee Proposals) and the engineering fee structure page at engg.yes.edu.in/engineering-fee-structures. Hostel fees, which include meals and accommodation, are published separately by the hostel office.

Because fees change year to year, the assistant never quotes fee amounts. The right next step is always to open the official fee page or ask the admission office to share the current FRA-approved structure for your programme. Fee payment can be completed online through the institute's secure payment portal, and there is a published fee refund policy on the website.

## Scholarships as per Government norms

Scholarships at Yashoda are available as per Government of Maharashtra and Government of India norms. The campus guides students through the applications. Schemes commonly used by Yashoda students include:

- Post-matric scholarships and freeships for SC, ST, OBC, VJNT and SBC category students through the MahaDBT portal (mahadbtmahait.gov.in)
- EBC (Economically Backward Class) fee concession — the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj scheme — for eligible families through MahaDBT
- Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) seats allotted during CAP rounds for eligible students
- Minority and national scholarships through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in)

Scholarship applications need supporting documents — caste and caste validity certificates, income certificate, domicile and bank details — so families are advised to keep these ready early. The admission office and the scholarship desk at the campus help students file applications correctly and on time.
