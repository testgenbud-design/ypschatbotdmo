## मराठीतून विचारले जाणारे प्रश्न (Marathi keywords for admissions)

Parents and students in Satara often ask in Marathi. These are the common terms and what they refer to at Yashoda Technical Campus:

- प्रवेश / ऍडमिशन — admission process, CAP rounds, institute-level seats
- प्रवेश प्रक्रिया — the admission process through the State CET Cell
- पात्रता — eligibility criteria for the course
- फी / फी स्ट्रक्चर — fees, the FRA-approved fee structure (always refer to the official fee page)
- अभ्यासक्रम — courses offered, B.Tech, M.Tech, B.Pharmacy, Polytechnic, MBA, MCA
- प्रवेश क्षमता / जागा — intake, seats available
- वसतिगृह / हॉस्टेल — hostel accommodation, girls hostel 350 and boys hostel 100 capacity
- मुलींचे वसतिगृह — girls hostel inside the campus
- मेस / जेवण — mess, dining hall, canteen, meals included in hostel fee
- बस सुविधा / वाहतूक — bus and transport facility from Satara district
- शिष्यवृत्ती — scholarship as per Government norms, MahaDBT, EBC, TFWS, freeship
- ईबीसी — EBC fee concession scheme of Government of Maharashtra
- दस्तऐवज / कागदपत्रे — documents required for admission
- कट ऑफ / गुण — cut-off and marks (the assistant never quotes these; the office confirms)
- नोकरी / प्लेसमेंट / कॅम्पस इंटरव्ह्यू — placements, training and placement cell, campus interviews
- पगार / पॅकेज — salary packages, highest 22 LPA and average 4 LPA as published by the campus
- कॉलेजचा पत्ता / कसे जायचे — address and how to reach, Wadhe NH-4 Satara
- संपर्क / फोन नंबर — contact, +91 91722 20775
- सातारा — the campus is at Wadhe near Satara city, Maharashtra
- रेंक / प्रमाणपत्र — NAAC B+ grade, NBA accreditation, autonomous status by UGC
- रॅगिंग — anti-ragging policy, grievance portal, antiragging.in

## हिन्दी में पूछे जाने वाले सवाल (Hindi keywords for admissions)

- एडमिशन / दाखिला — admission, the CAP process and institute-level admission
- पात्रता — eligibility for the chosen course
- फीस / शुल्क — fees, refer to the official FRA-approved fee page
- कोर्स / ब्रांच — courses and branches, engineering, pharmacy, polytechnic, MBA, MCA
- सीटें / इनटेक — seats and intake for each programme
- हॉस्टेल / छात्रावास — hostel for girls and boys inside the campus
- बस सुविधा — bus facility from Satara district
- छात्रवृत्ति / स्कॉलरशिप — scholarship through MahaDBT and the National Scholarship Portal
- दस्तावेज़ / कागजात — documents to keep ready for admission
- प्लेसमेंट / नौकरी — placements and campus interviews
- पता / कैसे पहुँचें — address at Wadhe, NH-4, Satara
- संपर्क नंबर — phone and WhatsApp +91 91722 20775
- दाखिला फॉर्म / आवेदन — how to apply, CAP registration and institute-level forms
- सीधे दूसरे वर्ष — direct second-year admission for diploma holders

## Hinglish short forms parents use

- "fees kitni hai" / "fee structure batao" — see the FRA fee page
- "admission kaise hota hai" — the CAP process explained above
- "hostel milta hai kya" — yes, girls 350 and boys 100 capacity in campus
- "scholarship milegi kya" — as per Government norms through MahaDBT
- "placement kaisa hai" — placement cell support, campus interviews; published highest 22 LPA, average 4 LPA
- "documents kya lagte hain" — the document list in the admission process page
- "college kahan hai" — Wadhe, NH-4, Satara, Maharashtra
