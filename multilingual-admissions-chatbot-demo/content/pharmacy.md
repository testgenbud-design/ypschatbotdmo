## Pharmacy courses and intake at Yashoda Faculty of Pharmacy

The Faculty of Pharmacy at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara is approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi and AICTE. It offers the full ladder of pharmacy education:

- Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharmacy) — affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, intake 60, started 2011, NBA accredited, choice code 675782310
- Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharmacy) — affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere, intake 100 plus 100, started 2011, NAAC accredited, choice code 675782310
- Pharm D — intake 30, started 2024, NAAC accredited, choice code 675710010

## M.Pharm specializations

Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharmacy) programmes are affiliated to DBATU, Lonere, with four specializations:

- Pharmaceutics — intake 15, started 2011
- Pharmacology — intake 9, started 2011
- Pharmaceutical Chemistry — intake 15, started 2024
- Regulatory Affairs — intake 15, started 2024

## Careers after pharmacy at Yashoda

Pharmacy graduates from the campus move into pharmaceutical industry roles, regulatory affairs, hospital and clinical pharmacy through the Pharm D route, and academia. Several alumni serve as assistant professors at pharmacy colleges — including the campus's own Faculty of Pharmacy. The Yashoda group also runs the Yashoda College of Pharmacy (ycop.edu.in) as a sister institute. The training and placement cell supports campus interviews and industrial exposure for pharmacy students.
