## Hostels at Yashoda

Yashoda Technical Campus provides hostel accommodation for girls and boys inside the green campus at Wadhe, Satara. The girls hostel is a safe and secure accommodation within the campus with a total intake capacity of 350; the boys hostel has a total intake capacity of 100. Hostel admission is done separately through the campus hostel office after admission to the course is confirmed.

Hostel facilities include a spacious and clean dining hall for hostellers, hygienic mess food, RO drinking water, first-aid facilities in each hostel, and access to the canteen, sports grounds and gymkhana. The hostel fee includes meals and accommodation; the current year's amounts are published by the hostel office, so please confirm the figure directly rather than relying on old numbers.

## Bus and transport facility

The campus runs a bus facility for students from across Satara district. The college website lists the bus facility from Satara district among its key facilities, and buses connect surrounding towns to the campus. Route and timing details for the current year are confirmed by the transport office at the time of admission.

## Where the campus is and how to reach it

Yashoda Technical Campus is located at Wadhe on National Highway 4 (NH-4, the Pune–Bengaluru highway), about 8 to 9 kilometres from the Satara bus stand on the Pune side. Typical travel times: from Pune about 110 km (2.5 to 3 hours by road); from Karad about 50 km (45 minutes); from Kolhapur about 130 km and Sangli about 120 km (about 2.5 hours); from Mumbai about 260 km (5 to 6 hours via the Mumbai–Pune Expressway and NH-4). The nearest airport is Pune International Airport (PNQ). MSRTC buses connect Satara with all major Maharashtra cities, and autos and cabs run from the Satara bus stand to the campus in 15 to 20 minutes.

## Campus life around the hostel

Life around the hostel is active: canteen, RO water and dining hall for daily needs; library and computer centre for study; sports infrastructure and gymkhana for recreation; auditorium events, cultural programmes and NSS activities through the year. The campus positions itself as a green, clean and healthy campus, and the girls hostel sits within the campus boundary for safety and convenience.
