## About the campus

Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara (known as YSPM's YTC) is run by the Yashoda Shikshan Prasarak Mandal (YSPM). The trust laid its foundation in the year 2009, so the campus carries a legacy of more than sixteen years of academic excellence. The campus is recognized by the Government of Maharashtra and approved by AICTE, New Delhi.

Yashoda Technical Campus has been conferred Autonomous Status by the University Grants Commission (UGC), India. The campus is accredited by NAAC with a 'B+' grade, and key programmes hold NBA accreditation.

## Scale of the campus

The campus is home to more than 5,000 students guided by over 200 staff members. The institute has built more than 300 industry connects and a global alumni family of over 50,000. Over the years the campus has received more than 30 awards, including honours such as the Lokmat Global Excellence Award.

## Affiliations and approvals at Yashoda

Programmes at Yashoda run under different affiliating bodies depending on the faculty:

- B.Tech, M.Tech, B.Pharmacy, M.Pharmacy and Pharm D are affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere.
- MBA and MCA are affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur.
- Diploma in Engineering (Polytechnic) and D.Pharmacy are affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai.
- Pharmacy programmes are approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi, and all technical programmes by AICTE and the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra.

## Faculties at Yashoda Technical Campus

The campus houses the Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Polytechnic, Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of MBA, Faculty of MCA, Faculty of BBA and Faculty of BCA. The Yashoda group also runs the Yashoda College of Architecture, Satara (ycoa.org.in) and the Yashoda College of Pharmacy (ycop.edu.in) as sister institutes.

## Campus environment

The campus describes itself as a green, clean and healthy campus. Facilities include well-equipped laboratories, a central library with digital resources, spacious smart classrooms, a central computer centre with high-speed internet, a large auditorium, canteen with hygienic food, RO drinking water, sports grounds, a gymkhana, and guest and expert lectures by eminent personalities. Students benefit from industrial tours and visits, campus interviews, and a dedicated training and placement cell.
