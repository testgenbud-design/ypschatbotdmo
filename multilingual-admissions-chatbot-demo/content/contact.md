## Contacting the admission office

The admission office at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara can be reached on phone and WhatsApp at +91 91722 20775, and by email at yspmadmin@yes.edu.in. Parents and students are welcome to call for any admission query — courses, intake, eligibility confirmation, fees (FRA-approved), hostel seats, bus routes or scholarship paperwork. The campus also runs an enquiry WhatsApp line on the same number listed on its website.

## Address and location

YSPM's Yashoda Technical Campus, Wadhe, on National Highway 4 (NH-4), Satara, Maharashtra. The campus is about 8–9 km from the Satara bus stand on the Pune side of the city. Visitors coming from Pune, Karad, Kolhapur or Sangli can follow NH-4 directly to the Wadhe campus.

## Office hours and campus visits

Families visiting Satara are welcome to see the campus at Wadhe — the admission office can arrange a campus tour covering laboratories, the library, hostels and the canteen. Calling ahead on +91 91722 20775 to fix a time is recommended, especially on weekends and during the CAP admission season.
