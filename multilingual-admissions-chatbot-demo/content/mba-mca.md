## MBA at Yashoda

The Faculty of MBA at Yashoda Technical Campus, Satara offers the Master of Business Administration (MBA), affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur. The programme is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and the Government of Maharashtra (DTE Mumbai), runs with an intake of 120 students, and began in 2011. Admission is through the MAH-MBA/MMS-CET followed by the centralised CAP rounds. The faculty runs professional development initiatives such as the T360 programme for students.

## MCA at Yashoda

The Faculty of MCA offers the Master in Computer Application (MCA), affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur, accredited by NAAC and NBA, with an intake of 180 students since 2011. Admission is through the MAH-MCA-CET and the CAP process. MCA students work in the central computer centre with high-speed internet and modern computing setups.

## BBA and BCA at Yashoda

The campus also houses the Faculty of BBA and the Faculty of BCA (undergraduate programmes affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur), with their own admission offices reachable through bba.yes.edu.in and bca.yes.edu.in. Families interested in undergraduate management or computer applications after 12th can ask the admission office about seats in these faculties.

## Architecture — a sister institute

The Yashoda group runs the Yashoda College of Architecture, Satara, as a separate institute on ycoa.org.in. Architecture admissions follow the NATA-based centralised process, and the college holds its own induction programmes on the group's campus in Satara. Questions about architecture admission are best confirmed with the architecture college office directly.
